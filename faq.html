Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about LIC policies, policy services and insurance planning.

Common Questions

Here are answers to some frequently asked questions.

How do I check my policy status?

You can check your policy status through applicable LIC customer service channels or contact me for guidance regarding the appropriate process.

How can I change my nominee?

Nominee changes may be possible subject to the applicable LIC rules and policy requirements. Contact me for guidance on the process and required documents.

What happens if my policy has lapsed?

Depending on the policy and applicable conditions, revival options may be available. Contact me to understand the applicable revival process.

Can I update my address in my policy?

Eligible policyholders may request an address update through the applicable LIC servicing process. Guidance can be provided regarding the required procedure.

How can I get help with a claim?

You can contact me for guidance regarding the claim process and documents that may be required, subject to the applicable LIC policy terms and requirements.

Which LIC policy is suitable for me?

The suitable option depends on factors such as your age, financial goals, family responsibilities, budget and required protection. A personal discussion can help identify relevant policy categories.

Are there plans for children's future?

LIC offers policy solutions that can be considered for children's education and future financial planning. The appropriate option depends on the child's age, goal and policy terms.

Are there retirement and pension solutions?

Yes, LIC has policy categories designed for retirement and pension planning. The appropriate option depends on age, retirement goals and applicable policy terms.

Can I get help with an existing LIC policy?

Yes. Guidance is available for common policy servicing requirements such as nominee changes, address updates, policy status, revival and claim-related assistance.

How can I contact you?

You can use the Contact page to call, send a WhatsApp message or email regarding your LIC policy requirement.
